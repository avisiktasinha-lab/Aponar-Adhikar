Before you apply

Every scheme on this page is free to apply for, directly with the government. Middlemen who charge a "processing fee" or promise to "guarantee approval" are not part of any official process — and they are the exact problem this page exists to remove.

No fee

No official West Bengal or central scheme ever charges a fee to apply, check eligibility, or receive your card. If someone asks for money, that is not the government.

Apply direct

Use the official portal linked on each card, or visit a Duare Sarkar camp or Bangla Sahayta Kendra in person — both are free, government-run, and open to walk-ins.

Keep proof

Always keep a copy or screenshot of what you submit, and note your application or URN number. It is your only proof if a claim is delayed or disputed.

Report agents

If someone demands payment to process a government scheme, that can be reported to the local BDO office or the scheme\'s own helpline number.

Many of these schemes were only announced in West Bengal\'s May 2026 cabinet decisions and the June 2026 state budget, so exact amounts, start dates and application steps are still being finalised by departments. Always confirm the current details on the official portal linked before you apply. This page is independent and not run by any government body.
